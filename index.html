FAQ

Works in Google Sheets?

Yes — upload to Drive and open with Google Sheets. Formulas, dropdowns and alerts all work.

How is this different from property-management software?

PMS runs $20–50/month per unit and wants your bank login. RentSheet is a one-time $6.99 spreadsheet you own, offline-friendly, with no account to create.

How many units?

Up to 200 rows per sheet — most small landlords run 1–20 units comfortably.

Can I use it for Airbnb / short-term rentals?

Yes — log each payout as a rent row per property; expenses work the same.
